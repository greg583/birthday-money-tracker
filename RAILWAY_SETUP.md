# Birthday Money Tracker - Deploy to Railway

## Quick Setup (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Create a GitHub Account (if you don't have one)
- Go to github.com and sign up (free)

### Step 2: Create a GitHub Repository
1. Go to github.com/new
2. Name it: `birthday-money-tracker`
3. Click "Create repository"

### Step 3: Add Your Files to GitHub
1. In your new repository, click "Upload files"
2. Upload ALL these files:
   - `package.json`
   - `tailwind.config.js`
   - `.gitignore`
   - `app/page.jsx`
   - `app/layout.jsx`
   - `app/globals.css`
3. Click "Commit changes"

### Step 4: Deploy to Railway
1. Go to railway.app
2. Click "New Project"
3. Click "Deploy from GitHub"
4. Select your `birthday-money-tracker` repository
5. Click "Deploy"
6. Wait 2-3 minutes (it will build automatically)
7. You'll get a URL like: `your-app.railway.app`

### Step 5: Share Your Link!
- Copy the URL (e.g., `https://your-app.railway.app?event=xyz123`)
- Send it to family!

---

## That's it! 🎉

Your app is now live and will work for everyone who opens the link. No more "doesn't work" issues!

## How it works:
- Click "Create Event" to generate a unique link
- Share that link with family
- Everyone adds their name and cash amount
- Progress bar fills up toward $1000 goal
- Leaderboard shows who's bringing the most
- Data saves automatically (Firebase + local storage backup)

## Questions?
If Railway asks for a credit card, don't worry - it's free for this app. They just need it for security (won't charge you).
